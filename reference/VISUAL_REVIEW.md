## Corrections to earlier notes

- The modular reference has two round vertical uprights, not four. Four circular attachment locations are the upper/lower joints of those two posts.
- Images 01–04 and 05–11 show distinct architectures. The user selected the latter.
- The base opening serves the combined bottom battery/card compartment. There is no separate right-side media door to drive the design.
- The front silver adjustable stud is a lens-adapter support, confirmed in the [manufacturer's description](https://www.mid49.com/products/upper-cage-for-nikon-zr).
- A pictured hole pattern does not establish thread sizes or a locating-pin standard. Define each interface from a mating drawing/part.
- The white camera in these renders makes the black cage easy to inspect. Use a neutral camera reference in our assembly views too.

## Comparison method

Compare our next cage to 06, 10 and 11 at similar viewing angles: first the open silhouette, then contours/radii, post-to-clamp relationships, hole spacing and fasteners. Use 05 for underside access and 07–08 for rear/connectors. Use 02–04 for the detachable grip and secondary finish details. Generate all review renders from the CAD parts that will be exported.
